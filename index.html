Our Story

Forged in the
Valley

Founded in Srinagar in 2008 by Mir Mohammad Mohsin, MIRZ Infrastructure Builders was born from a simple ambition: to build infrastructure that matches the resilience of Kashmir itself. From our early days on rural road projects in Ganderbal, we have grown into one of the valley's most trusted civil contractors.

We specialize in civil infrastructure across the entirety of Jammu & Kashmir — executing complex projects in high-altitude terrain and extreme weather conditions that would ground lesser teams. Every bridge, road, and structure we deliver is engineered to endure decades of Himalayan winters.

As a government-approved contractor registered with J&K PWD and PMGSY, we bring institutional trust, transparent processes, and a proven track record spanning 8 districts to every engagement.

What We Build

Our Services

From national highway expansion to slope stabilization in remote valleys, we deliver across the full spectrum of civil infrastructure.

🛣️

Road & Highway Construction

National highways, district roads, and rural connectivity — engineered for Kashmir's challenging terrain and sub-zero winters. Compliant with PMGSY and NHAI standards.

🌉

Bridge & Culvert Development

RCC and steel bridges designed for high flood discharge and seismic zones. We've commissioned structures spanning major rivers across the valley.

🏠

Residential Buildings

From affordable housing clusters to quality residential complexes — built with certified materials, engineered for energy efficiency in extreme cold.

🏢

Commercial Complexes

Multi-storey commercial, retail, and office structures designed for modern standards. Full MEP coordination and project management included.

🏛️

Government Infrastructure

Schools, hospitals, public facilities, and institutional buildings executed under government contract with full transparency and audit compliance.

⛰️

Retaining Walls & Slope Stabilization

Geotechnical solutions for landslide-prone Himalayan terrain — gabion walls, RCC retaining structures, and drainage engineering.
